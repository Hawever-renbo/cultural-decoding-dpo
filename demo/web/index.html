Three levels, one page.

IOpen codes. Each statement gets a short label in the speaker's own words, tied to a verbatim quote. This is the step the post-trained model does. Program checks flag any word the speaker never used.

II–IVConcepts, categories, core. A general model abstracts upward under fixed floors: six paradigm slots, no bare-noun names, a core category that stays on your focus. Rounds are ranked by program, not by a model's opinion of itself.

V–VIDecoding and checks. A thick-description reading that may quote only what was said, and a ledger of the numbers behind the run.
